# Inter font files

Place self-hosted Inter woff2 files here before deploying.

Required files:
- Inter-Regular.woff2   (weight 400)
- Inter-Medium.woff2    (weight 500)
- Inter-SemiBold.woff2  (weight 600)
- Inter-Bold.woff2      (weight 700)

Download from: https://rsms.me/inter/
Or use `fontsource` to download: https://fontsource.org/fonts/inter

Choose the Latin subset to keep file size down.
Self-hosting is required for DSGVO compliance — do NOT load from Google's CDN.
